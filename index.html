Find Your Level

Power 4 Test

Warm up, then do max reps of each with good form, resting 2 min between. Not pass/fail — it just sets your start line.

1. Max Pull-Ups → 2. Max Squats → 3. Max Push-Ups → 4. Max Plank hold
