Effort Guide

Easy — full sentences, nose-breathing (zone 2)
Tempo — comfortably hard, a few words at a time
Hard — ~5K effort, breathing heavy but controlled
Strides — 15–20 sec smooth & fast, full recovery

Sidewalk Tips

No track needed — mark intervals by mailboxes, poles, driveways or blocks. The clock keeps running through crossings; it's effort and time, not perfect distance. Face traffic where there's no sidewalk, and wear reflective gear or a headlamp in the dark. For time trials, a phone app (Strava, Nike Run, Garmin) measures the 2 miles — or map a 1-mile out-and-back once and reuse it.

Bodyweight-Day Cardio

End each bodyweight day with its cardio line: sprints · jump rope · shadow boxing · stairs · ruck · bag work · a sport.

Benchmark

Power 4 Test

Warm up, then do max reps of each with good form, resting 2 min between. Re-test after the block to measure the jump.

1. Max Pull-Ups → 2. Max Squats → 3. Max Push-Ups → 4. Max Plank hold

The 2-Mile

· Wk 1 Sat — baseline 2-mile time trial, all-out
· Tue — intervals build 2-mile pace
· Sat — easy base + tempo build the engine
· Wk 7 Sat — final 2-mile, compare the gap
· Log each run's time in the box on that day
